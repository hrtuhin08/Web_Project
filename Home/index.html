About UIU Hostels

A Disciplined, Hygienic & Scholarly Living Space

Mess Automation

Transparent, Food-Waste-Free Dining

Our intelligent mess management system eliminates food waste and guarantees complete billing transparency.

⏰

Strict 9:00 PM Meal Lock

Boarders must toggle their attendance for Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner before 9:00 PM every night. The kitchen cooks exactly according to confirmed headcounts to maintain supreme hygiene and eliminate food waste.

📊

Dynamic Meal Cost Formula

Meal rate is calculated dynamically from real market expenses: Rate = Total Bazar Expense ÷ Total Consumed Meals. All invoices and receipts are published daily on the boarder portal.

🍲

7-Day Rotating Menu

A balanced weekly menu featuring beef, chicken, fresh fish (Rui, Hilsha, Katla), seasonal vegetables, lentils, and dessert. Students review and rate meals directly from their dashboard.
